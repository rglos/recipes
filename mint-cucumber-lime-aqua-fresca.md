# Mint Cucumber Lime Aqua Fresca

## Ingredients

 - cucumber
 - water
 - lime juice
 - agave syrup

## Ratio

| Ingredient    | 2 Serving - Grams     | 3 Serving - Grams     | 4 Serving - Grams     |
| -----------   |-------                | -------               | -------               |
| cucumber      | 240g                  | 360g                  | 480g                  |
| water         | 240g                  | 360g                  | 480g                  |
| lime juice    | 180g                  | 270g                  | 360g                  |
| agave syrup   | 90g                   | 135g                  | 180g                  |
