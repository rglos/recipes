# Chili Crisp Dressing

## Ingredients

Base dressing

- 80 ml nut butter (peanut, almond, cashew, etc)
- 80 ml soy sauce
- 80 ml Chinese black vinegar (or 50/50 of balsamic vinegar and rice vinegar) - or any vinegar 
- 30 ml rice wine vinegar
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 10 ml grated fresh ginger
- 10 ml white sugar
- 15 ml toasted sesame oil

To loosen the dressing

- 80 ml hot noodle water - add to base dressing and whisk until smooth as hot water helps with the nut butter

Heat & flavor

- 80 ml chili crisp

Notes

- Peanut butter thickness varies — if your dressing feels tight, add 10–20 ml more noodle water until glossy.
- If you want a lighter, more pourable dressing (especially for salad applications), increase the noodle water to 100–120 ml.
- If you want more punch, add an extra 10–20 ml chili crisp.
