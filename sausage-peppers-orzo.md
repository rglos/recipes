# Sausage, Peppers and Orzo - Everything Cooks in One Pan

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 pounds (570g) Italian sausage - 1 lb to 1.5 lbs - Can use bulk or links - mild or hot depending on how you are feeling
- 1/3 cup (80ml) extra virgin olive oil
- 1 large red bell pepper - chopped
- 1 large green bell pepper - chopped
- 1 large onion chopped
- 1/2 cup (120ml) water
- 10 cloves garlic - chopped
- 1/3 cup (80g) tomato paste
- 4 cups (960ml) low-sodium chicken stock
- 2 teaspoons Diamond crystal kosher salt
- 2 teaspoons fennel seeds
- 1 tablespoon dried Sicilian oregano or dried Italian oregano
- salt and pepper - to taste
- 1 pound (454g) orzo pasta
- 1/4 cup basil leaves - hand torn

## Instructions

- Add olive oil and cook the sausage in a large pot over medium heat until browned. Remove the sausage from the pot and set aside.
- Add the peppers and onions to the pot and cook until softened. Add water and cover for 5 minutes to help them cook faster.  Remove lid and boil off the water.  
- Make space in middle, add garlic and a little olive oil and cook until fragrant and slightly toasted
- Add tomato paste and cook for few minutes
- Add salt, fennel seeds, oregano, and pepper.  
- Add orzo and stir to coat with the tomato paste and spices.  
- Add back sausage and stir to combine.  
- Add chicken stock and bring to a simmer.  
- Reduce heat to low, cover and simmer for 15 minutes to cook the orzo.  Stir occasionally to prevent sticking.
- Remove from heat and let sit for 5 minutes.  
- Stir in basil and serve topped with parmesan cheese.
